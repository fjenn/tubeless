3

Use a bookmark

2

Tubeless

3

Open YouTube

4

Good to know

Tubeless runs in your own browser and uses the YouTube account you're signed in with there, like youtube.com itself. Nothing goes through us: only YouTube and the services the app already uses (SponsorBlock, Return YouTube Dislike). The bookmark always loads the newest Tubeless. To get a newer extension, download it again here and replace the folder. To use it signed out, turn off Settings → Account → "Use my youtube.com sign-in". The source is public.

The Tubeless apps for Mac, Windows, Linux, iPhone and Android are coming to this site soon.

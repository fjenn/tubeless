Add Tubeless to Chrome

Downloads a small zip file. Then four quick steps:

1

Unzip the downloaded Tubeless.zip: double-click it. You get a Tubeless folder. Keep it, for example in Documents.

2

Copy this address into a new tab and press Enter:

chrome://extensions

3

Turn on Developer mode (top right).

4

Click Load unpacked and choose the Tubeless folder. Done: click the Tubeless icon next to the address bar to open it.

Good to know

Tubeless runs in your own browser and uses the YouTube account you're signed in with there, like youtube.com itself. Nothing goes through us: only YouTube and the services the app already uses (SponsorBlock, Return YouTube Dislike). The bookmark loads the newest Tubeless each time; the extension updates when you add the new one. To use it signed out, turn off Settings → Account → "Use my youtube.com sign-in". The source is public.

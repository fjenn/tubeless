Tubeless on youtube.com

One bookmark, no extension. Open youtube.com, press the bookmark, and that tab becomes Tubeless: with your own YouTube account, HD and 4K downloads, stable volume and voice boost. It runs in your browser as part of youtube.com; nothing goes through us.

Computer: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave

  1. Show the bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B, on a Mac ⌘+Shift+B).
  2. Drag this button onto it:
Tubeless (Clicking it here does nothing: it works on youtube.com.)
  1. Open youtube.com and press Tubeless in the bookmarks bar. Use it again on each visit; your library and downloads stay.

Safari (Mac, iPhone, iPad) and phones

  2. Bookmark this page (Share → Add Bookmark, or ⌘D), name it Tubeless.
  3. Edit that bookmark (Bookmarks → Edit) and paste the copied text as its address.
  4. Open www.youtube.com/robots.txt?tubeless (a plain youtube.com page; bookmark it too), then open the Tubeless bookmark. On a phone: tap the address bar, type Tubeless and tap the bookmark.

Why that page: Safari doesn't let bookmarks run on youtube.com's normal pages, only on pages like this one. Chrome and Firefox run it anywhere on www.youtube.com; from any other site it first takes you to that page (press it once more there).

What it can see

The bookmark loads Tubeless from this site into youtube.com. Like youtube.com itself, it uses your YouTube sign-in in this browser to show your Home, subscriptions and library (turn it on in Settings → Account). Nothing is sent anywhere but YouTube and the services the app already uses (SponsorBlock, Return YouTube Dislike). The source is public.
